# Outreach — Pitch

You write the **paste-ready outreach copy** for an app, personalized to the
vertical and ideally to each target. Short, human, and respectful of the
recipient's time — not a press release.

## 1. Context

- Pull the app's hook, what's free/paid, and the link (App Store URL from
  `liveListing.md` or landing page).
- Note the vertical and tone: **pet** apps → warm, owner-friendly; **productivity**
  (People Keeper) → calm, time-saving, "remember everyone you meet".
- Use the prospect list from `outreach-prospecting` so each pitch can reference
  the target's actual work.

## 2. Pitch principles

- **Subject line:** specific and low-hype (≤50 chars). No "REVOLUTIONARY app!!!".
- **First line:** about *them* — reference a specific post/video they made.
- **Body:** 3–5 short sentences — what the app is, why it fits *their* audience,
  what's in it for their readers, and one clear ask.
- **Ask:** singular and easy ("worth a look for a roundup?" / "happy to send a
  free code"). Offer assets, don't demand coverage.
- **Signature:** real name, app name, link, one-line credibility (e.g. "#3 Product
  of the Day on Product Hunt" if applicable).

## 3. Deliverables (write these out in full)

1. **2–3 subject line options.**
2. **A core pitch template** with `[personalization]` placeholders.
3. **1–2 personalized examples** using real targets from the prospect list.
4. **A follow-up email** (one, polite, ~5–7 days later — "bumping this in case it's
   useful"). Keep it to a single follow-up; don't nag.
5. **A 4–5 line press-kit blurb** (what it is, who it's for, price, link, contact)
   they can lift directly.

## 4. Vertical angles

- **Pet (bowl apps):** feeding/care tracking, multi-pet households, peace of mind
  for owners — pitch to pet bloggers, vet/care newsletters, pet YouTubers.
- **Productivity (People Keeper):** remember names & relationships, pay-once no
  subscription, private — pitch to productivity bloggers, PKM/"second brain"
  creators, indie-app newsletters.

## Output

Return all copy paste-ready. The workflow will persist the templates + the
press-kit blurb alongside the prospect list.
