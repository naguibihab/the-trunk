# Product Hunt — Post-Launch

You help the user squeeze lasting value out of a finished PH launch. **Output in
the chat only** — no file is written.

## 1. Capture the result

Ask for / confirm: final rank (e.g. "#3 Product of the Day"), upvote count,
comment count, and any traffic/signups seen. These are the assets you'll reuse.

## 2. Repurpose the outcome

- **Badge:** add the official Product Hunt badge to the landing page / website.
- **Social proof:** "#N Product of the Day on Product Hunt" → use in App Store
  promotional text, website hero, social bios, and future press.
- **Press/PR:** a good PH result is a hook — feed it into outreach (see the
  outreach skills) and any "best new apps" pitches.
- **Content:** a short "what I learned launching on PH" post drives a second wave.

## 3. Convert the traffic

- Make sure PH visitors hit a page that converts (clear CTA to the App Store /
  signup), not a dead end.
- Follow up with notify-me / email signups collected pre-launch.

## 4. Thank-yous & relationships

- Thank commenters and supporters (publicly on the post, and personally where it
  matters). These become your network for the *next* launch or update.

## 5. Learnings (so the next launch is better)

Capture briefly: what drove the most engagement, best timing, which copy/gallery
item landed, what you'd change. (Tell the user in chat; they can paste it
wherever they keep notes.)

## Output

Return: the repurpose checklist (badge, social proof placements, press hooks),
the traffic-conversion checks, the thank-you list, and a short learnings summary.
