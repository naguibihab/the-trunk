# Product Hunt — Launch Day

You run the launch-day playbook with the user. **Give a tight, time-ordered
plan and paste-ready reply templates in the chat.** No file output — it's a
one-day event.

## Go-live (first 30 minutes)

1. Post goes live (or hunter posts) at **12:01 AM PST**.
2. Maker posts the **first comment** immediately (from `ph-launch-prep`).
3. Notify your own channels with the **direct link**: email list, social, any
   communities you're already a member of. Share the *post*, not "please upvote".
4. Double-check gallery, links, and that the App Store/landing URL works.

## Engagement rules (important — don't get penalized)

- **Never ask for upvotes** anywhere (PH detects and penalizes vote manipulation).
  Ask people to "check it out" / "share feedback" — let votes be organic.
- No vote rings, no incentivized votes, no buying votes.
- Reply to **every comment**, fast, in your own voice — especially in the first
  few hours when ranking momentum is set.
- Be gracious to critics; thank them and note what you'll consider.

## Cadence through the day

| Window | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 0–4 hrs | Highest priority — reply to all comments within minutes, share to your channels. |
| Midday | Second push to your network (different timezone), answer questions, post an update if there's a milestone. |
| Evening | Keep replying; thank supporters; PST day ends at 11:59 PM. |

## Reply templates (adapt voice)

- **"Congrats / looks great"** → thank them + one specific detail + invite to try it.
- **Feature question** → answer directly + link to where it's shown.
- **Comparison to X** → acknowledge X, state your honest differentiator (no trashing).
- **Criticism / bug** → thank, acknowledge, say what you'll do, follow up later.

## Output

Return: the hour-by-hour checklist for *today*, the engagement do/don't list,
and the reply templates filled in for this product. Hand off to
`ph-post-launch` once the day closes.
