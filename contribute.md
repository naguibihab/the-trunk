# Contribute

This repo shares **The Tree**: the garden shape and the **skills**. Project source lives in **private sibling repos** (`the-sprouts`, `limb-*`, `the-saplings`). Do not send your apps here. Do not expect to see mine.

## What to contribute

A sharper or new `SKILL.md` under `.cursor/skills/`.

## What not to send

- Limb source, sprout briefs from your garden, sapling sites
- Secrets, `.env`, store or host tokens

Use the skills in your own trunk. `apps.json` on a private checkout may point at `../the-sprouts/` and `../limb-*`; that fruit stays off the contribution surface.
