# Trail Log

Sample **growing** app. Numbers below are invented so the dashboard has something to show. Replace this folder with a real limb, or delete the row.

## Problem

Hikers lose the route they just walked because notes live in a camera roll and a separate map app.

## Proposed solution

- Log a walk, a few photos, and a short note in one place.
- TestFlight only. No App Store listing and no paywall yet.
